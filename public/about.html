Buying a car should be simple

We help you find, finance and drive home the right car. We deal with the sellers and the paperwork for you.

Our story

Built by drivers who were tired of the runaround.

AutoMart started in 2019 with a simple frustration: buying a car meant weekends lost to haggling, vague histories and piles of paperwork. So we rebuilt the process around the buyer.

Today our specialists inspect every car on 200+ checkpoints, negotiate with sellers on your behalf and arrange financing in hours, not weeks.

  • Transparent, no-haggle pricing
  • Full history & document verification
  • 7-day money-back guarantee
