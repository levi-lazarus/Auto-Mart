FAQ

Questions, answered

What does the 200-point inspection cover?

Our certified technicians check the engine, transmission, brakes, suspension, electrics, bodywork, interior and a full road test. You receive the complete report before you commit.

Can I get a car loan with less-than-perfect credit?

Yes. We work with a panel of lenders and can usually find an option. Pre-approval uses a soft credit check, so it never affects your score.

How does the 7-day money-back guarantee work?

Drive the car for up to 7 days or 300 miles. If it's not right, bring it back for a full refund.

Do you deliver?

We deliver nationwide, usually within 48 hours of your paperwork being completed. Local delivery within 30 miles is free.

Can I make an offer on a car?

Absolutely. Open any car in our inventory, enter your offer and a manager will respond within two hours.
